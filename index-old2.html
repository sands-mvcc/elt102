Digital Logic & Solid State Devices

A 14-day intensive journey from semiconductor fundamentals through digital logic systems — combining lecture, hands-on lab work, and practical application for careers in electronics and technology.

📐 2 hrs Lecture / 2 hrs Lab per session ⚗️ 10 Lab Exercises 📝 6 Module Exams + Final 🏫 MVCC Dept. of Engineering & Technology
🔌 Days 1–7: Solid State Devices & Analog Circuits
💡 Days 8–14: Digital Logic & Integrated Circuits

Phase 1 — Solid State Devices & Analog Circuits

Days 1–7
Day 1 Analog
Semiconductor Fundamentals & Diodes
Atomic structure of semiconductor materials, P-N junction behavior, forward and reverse bias, and diode characteristic curves.
P-N Junction Forward Bias Reverse Bias Depletion Region
Lab 1A Part 1 — Diode Biasing
→
Day 2 Analog
Diode Applications & Power Supply Circuits
Half-wave and full-wave rectifier circuits, capacitive filter networks, ripple voltage, and oscilloscope measurement of AC/DC conversion.
Rectification Full-Wave Bridge Ripple Voltage Capacitor Filter
Lab 1A Part 2 & Lab 1B — Oscilloscope
→
Day 3 Analog
Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJTs)
NPN and PNP transistor operation, three BJT configurations (Common Emitter, Base, Collector), and DC load line analysis for bias calculation.
Transistor Saturation Cutoff Active Region
📝 Exam 1 Lab 2A — BJT Switch
→
Day 4 Analog
Field Effect Transistors & Thyristors
JFETs vs. MOSFETs (voltage-controlled devices), SCR and TRIAC triggering and conduction, and high-power switching applications.
JFET MOSFET Thyristor SCR TRIAC
Lab 2A Extended — FET Characteristics
→
Day 5 Analog
Transistor Amplifiers & Op-Amps
Small-signal AC amplifiers, voltage gain and impedance analysis for common-emitter circuits, and introduction to Op-Amp inverting/non-inverting configurations.
AC Gain Coupling Capacitor Op-Amp Differential Gain
Lab 2B Part 1 — Amplifiers & Regulators
→
Day 6 Analog
Voltage Regulators & Optoelectronics
Linear voltage regulators under load, 78XX series IC regulators, LEDs, photodiodes, optocouplers, and optical isolation circuit design.
Voltage Regulation Zener Diode Optocoupler LED
Lab 2B Part 2 — Regulated Power Supply
→
Day 7 Midpoint
Midterm Review & Transition to Digital
Synthesize analog/solid-state concepts, analyze integration of components into digital architectures, and bridge transistor switching to logic gate operation.
Hybrid Circuits Interfacing Solid-State Integration
📝 Exam 2 Hardware Troubleshooting Lab
→

Phase 2 — Digital Logic & Integrated Circuits

Days 8–14
Day 8 Digital
Number Systems & Boolean Algebra
Binary, octal, decimal, and hexadecimal conversions, arithmetic in base-N, signed binary (2's complement), and fundamental Boolean algebra laws.
Binary Radix Hexadecimal 2's Complement Boolean Expression
Lab 3A & 3B — Binary, Octal & Hex Computation
→
Day 9 Digital
Fundamental Logic Gates
Truth tables and schematic symbols for AND, NAND, OR, NOR gates; universal gate demonstration; 7400-series TTL/CMOS IC datasheet analysis and verification.
Truth Table Universal Gate NAND NOR Propagation Delay
📝 Exam 3 Lab 4A & 4B — AND/NAND, OR/NOR ICs
→
Day 10 Digital
Advanced Logic & Combinational Circuits
XOR and XNOR gate functionality, combinational circuit design from truth tables, Karnaugh Map minimization, and multi-gate fault isolation techniques.
XOR XNOR Combinational Logic Parity Generator K-Map
Lab 4C — XOR & XNOR Gates
→
Day 11 Digital
Latches & Flip-Flops
Async latches vs. synchronous flip-flops, timing diagrams and state transitions for RS, D-type, and JK flip-flops, clock setup/hold times, and race condition resolution.
Clock Signal Edge-Triggered RS Latch D Flip-Flop JK Flip-Flop
📝 Exam 4 Lab 5A, 5B, 5C — RS/D/JK Flip-Flops
→
Day 12 Digital
Counters, Timers & Oscillators
Asynchronous (ripple) vs. synchronous Up/Down counters, 555 Timer IC in astable and monostable modes, frequency and duty cycle calculations.
Synchronous Counter Modulus 555 Timer Astable Duty Cycle
📝 Exam 5 Lab 6A & 6B — Counters & 555 Timers
→
Day 13 Digital
Data Conversion: Encoders, Decoders, MUX & A/D–D/A
Multiplexers, demultiplexers, encoders/decoders, BCD-to-7-segment display interfacing, successive approximation ADC, and R-2R ladder DAC principles.
MUX Encoder/Decoder BCD Seven-Segment ADC DAC
📝 Exam 6 Lab 7A, 7B, 7C — BCD, 7-Seg, ADC/DAC
→
Day 14 Final
Course Review & Final Examination
Comprehensive integration review of solid-state components, analog infrastructure, and digital processing workflows — device behaviors, troubleshooting, and digital/analog interfaces.
Solid-State Electronics Digital Systems System Integration
📝 Final Exam — Comprehensive (Online, Open-Notes)
→
