    🛠️ What a Junior Technician Needs to Know

    AreaThe technician can…Practice it
    Ideal vs. real op-ampState the ideal characteristics (infinite gain and input impedance, zero output impedance) and the real limits that matter on the bench: ±Vsat, gain-bandwidth product, slew rate, input offset.Labs 3, 9, 10 with “Real 741 behavior” on
    Packages and powerIdentify 741 and LM358 pinouts, wire dual and single supplies, and recognize decoupling capacitors and offset-null pins.Bench / Tinkercad op-amp lab
    Golden rules and virtual groundUse “no input current” and “inputs equal under negative feedback” to explain any configuration, and expect about 0 V at the − pin of a working inverting stage.Inverting and non-inverting modes
    Gain calculationsCalculate inverting and non-inverting gain from resistor values (including the color code and E12 values) and predict output amplitude and phase.Labs 1, 2, 4
    Configuration recognitionName a circuit on sight by where the signal enters and what sits in the feedback path: resistor, wire, capacitor, or nothing.Live schematic panel
    ComparatorsSet a reference, predict output state and duty cycle, and explain chatter and the need for hysteresis.Labs 5, 6
    Integrators and differentiatorsRecognize square↔triangle conversions, explain why practical circuits add a bleed resistor or a series input resistor, and find the corner frequency.Labs 7, 8
    Test equipmentSet a function generator; set V/div, time/div and triggering on a scope; measure peak, peak-to-peak, gain and phase.Scope display and measurement row
    TroubleshootingRead the symptom: output stuck at a rail (open feedback), no output (open input or shorted feedback), flattened peaks (clipping), trapezoids (slew), wrong gain (wrong resistor).Lab 10 and the fault menu
    Documentation and safetyPull key specs from a datasheet, handle ICs with ESD precautions, and record measured against expected values.Bench / lab report
