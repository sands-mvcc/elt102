Course Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of ELT 102-350, students will be able to:

Student Learning Outcomes (SLOs)

  • 1 Explain semiconductor physics including atomic structure, doping, and P-N junction behavior to analyze diode operation in forward and reverse bias conditions.
  • 2 Design and analyze diode circuits including half-wave and full-wave rectifiers, capacitive filter networks, and voltage regulator stages, and measure waveforms using an oscilloscope.
  • 3 Describe the operating characteristics of optoelectronic devices (LEDs, photodiodes, optocouplers) and select appropriate devices for optical isolation applications.
  • 4 Analyze BJT, JFET, MOSFET, SCR, and TRIAC operation in switching and amplification circuits, applying DC load line analysis and biasing calculations.
  • 5 Design and evaluate small-signal transistor amplifier circuits and operational amplifier configurations, calculating voltage gain, input/output impedance, and bandwidth.
  • 6 Perform conversions between binary, octal, decimal, and hexadecimal number systems, apply signed binary arithmetic using 2's complement, and solve Boolean algebra expressions.
  • 7 Design and minimize combinational logic circuits using truth tables, Boolean simplification, and Karnaugh Maps, and implement designs using standard gate ICs.
  • 8 Analyze sequential logic circuits including SR, D, and JK flip-flops, and apply them to the design of registers, latches, and state machines.
  • 9 Construct and test encoder, decoder, multiplexer, and A/D–D/A converter circuits, explaining the role of each in data conversion and digital communications systems.
  • 10 Build and measure asynchronous/synchronous counter circuits and 555-based timer/oscillator circuits, calculating frequency, duty cycle, and RC time constants.
  • 11 Demonstrate safe lab practices, accurate circuit construction on breadboards, and correct use of multimeters, oscilloscopes, and function generators to troubleshoot electronic circuits.

Lab Activities

Eight hands-on lab exercises covering circuit construction, measurement, and analysis. Each lab is linked to its corresponding lesson and requires use of the Electronics Lab Kit.

Lab 1 · Semiconductor Devices
⚡
Diode Operations
Plot forward and reverse bias characteristic curves for Si and Ge diodes. Measure threshold voltage, dynamic resistance, and observe breakdown behavior using the DC power supply and DMM.
Skills: DMM · Power Supply · Curve Tracing · Forward/Reverse Bias
→
Lab 2 · Rectifier Circuits
🔌
Rectifier & Power Supply Analysis
Build and compare half-wave and full-wave bridge rectifier circuits. Add capacitive filter stages and measure ripple voltage with the oscilloscope. Verify zener regulation performance.
Skills: Oscilloscope · Bridge Rectifier · Filter · Ripple · Zener
→
Lab 3 · BJT Transistors
🔋
BJT Biasing & DC Load Line
Bias an NPN transistor in the common-emitter configuration. Plot the DC load line on the collector characteristics, identify the Q-point, and verify saturation vs. cutoff switching behavior.
Skills: BJT Biasing · Load Line · Q-Point · CE Configuration · Switching
→
Lab 4 · Operational Amplifiers & Op-Amps
📡
Common-Emitter Amplifier & Op-Amp Configurations
Measure voltage gain, input/output impedance, and phase inversion for a CE amplifier. Then configure an LM741 op-amp as both inverting and non-inverting amplifiers and verify gain equations.
Skills: Gain Measurement · Impedance · CE Amp · Op-Amp · Inverting/Non-Inverting
→
Lab 5 · Logic Gates
🔢
Logic Gate Truth Table Verification
Wire AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, XOR, and XNOR gates using 7400-series ICs. Complete full truth tables using LED output indicators and verify Boolean identities with multi-gate combinations.
Skills: 7400 ICs · Truth Tables · Logic Probe · Boolean Verification
→
Lab 6 · Latches and Flip-Flops
🔄
Flip-Flop Operation & Register Design
Construct and test SR, D, and JK flip-flop circuits using 74xx ICs. Demonstrate clocked operation, set/reset, and toggle modes. Chain flip-flops to build a 4-bit shift register.
Skills: D Flip-Flop · JK Flip-Flop · Clock Signal · Shift Register
→
Lab 7 · Timers, Cscillators and Counters
🔁
Binary & Decade Counter Circuits
Build a 4-bit ripple counter using JK flip-flops and a synchronous decade counter using a 74160 IC. Observe waveforms with the oscilloscope and calculate count frequency vs. clock input.
Skills: Ripple Counter · Synchronous Counter · 74160 · Frequency · Waveform Analysis
→
Lab 8 · Encoders & Decoders Muxes and Demuxs and A-to-D and D-to-A Converts
🖵
7-Segment Display Decoder & Priority Encoder
Wire a BCD-to-7-segment decoder IC to drive a common-cathode display. Design and test a priority encoder that translates switch inputs to a 3-bit binary output code.
Skills: BCD Decoder · 7-Segment Display · Priority Encoder · IC Wiring
→
